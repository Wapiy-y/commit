How We Protect It

Bill names and notes are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before being stored. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and cannot be recovered. Even with direct database access, sensitive fields are unreadable.

How We Use It

  • We do not sell or share your data
  • We do not use it for advertising
